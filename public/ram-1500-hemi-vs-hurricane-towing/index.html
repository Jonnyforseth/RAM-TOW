HEMI VS. HURRICANE TOWING

Compare configurations, not just engine badges.

The HEMI V8, Hurricane SO, and Hurricane HO are distinct RAM 1500 powertrains, but engine choice alone still does not provide an exact towing answer. The active RAM chart also depends on drivetrain, axle ratio, cab, bed, GVWR, and hitch type.

HEMI V8 towing

HEMI-equipped RAM 1500 ratings differ by axle, cab, bed, and drivetrain. A 4x2 maximum should not be used to rate a 4x4 truck.

Hurricane SO towing

Hurricane SO trucks have their own chart rows. Verify the exact engine wording and other configuration details through the VIN lookup.

Hurricane HO and specialty trims

Hurricane HO vehicles and specialty trims can have different capability limits. RAM Tow uses the sticker trim when it is available, but the actual chart match remains the deciding step.

Payload decides more than many expect

Even if a configuration clears trailer weight, its payload must also carry loaded tongue weight, people, cargo, and hitch equipment.

Run the VIN before purchase or towing, then confirm the final result against the truck's payload label and official RAM materials.
