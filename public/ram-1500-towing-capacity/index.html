RAM 1500 TOWING CAPACITY

The exact VIN matters.

RAM 1500 towing capacity varies by model year, engine, axle ratio, 4x2 or 4x4 drivetrain, cab, bed, GVWR, receiver-hitch guidance, and the trailer's loaded tongue weight. A published maximum is not the rating for every RAM 1500.

Engine changes the chart row

Pentastar V6, HEMI V8, Hurricane SO, and Hurricane HO engines are separate towing configurations. Use the engine stated on the sticker and the active model-year chart.

4x4 is not the same as 4x2

A tow number shown for a 4x2 RAM 1500 cannot be assumed for a 4x4. Always match the truck's drivetrain, cab, bed, and axle ratio to the right chart row.

Payload can be the limiter

Loaded tongue weight, passengers, cargo, hitch equipment, and accessories reduce available payload. Confirm the yellow payload label on the specific truck before towing.

Hitch type and loading matter

Follow RAM's hitch guidance and receiver limits. Use loaded trailer weight rather than dry weight, then check the actual tongue weight and payload together.
