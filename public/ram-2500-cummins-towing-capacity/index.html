Trailer capacity and payload must both work.

A Cummins-powered RAM 2500 may be capable of pulling a substantial trailer, but pin or tongue weight, passengers, cargo, hitch equipment, axle ratio, drivetrain, and the actual payload label can still control whether it is the right truck.

Start with loaded weight

Use the trailer's ready-to-camp or ready-to-work weight, not brochure dry weight. Enter the real tongue or pin weight as well.

Pin weight consumes payload

Fifth-wheel and gooseneck pin weight counts against the truck's payload just like passengers, cargo, and installed accessories.

Axle ratio and body configuration

The cab, bed, drivetrain, axle ratio, and GVWR place the truck in a specific RAM chart row. The VIN lookup is designed to identify those details from the available window sticker.

Verify the exact truck

Do not use a maximum published Cummins rating for every truck. Confirm the candidate VIN, door labels, hitch equipment, and payload sticker before purchase or towing.
