CAN A RAM 1500 TOW 10,000 LBS?

Some can. The VIN decides.

A 10,000 lb trailer is within reach for certain RAM 1500 configurations, but it is not a blanket rating for every truck. Engine, axle ratio, 4x2 or 4x4 drivetrain, cab, bed, GVWR, payload, receiver-hitch limits, and the trailer’s loaded tongue weight all need to line up.

RAM 1500 HEMI towing capacity

Hemi-equipped RAM 1500s can have very different ratings depending on drivetrain, axle ratio, cab, and bed. A rating that applies to a 4x2 cannot automatically be used for a 4x4. Run the exact VIN before buying or hitching up.

RAM 1500 Hurricane towing capacity

Hurricane SO and Hurricane HO trucks are different powertrain families with distinct chart rows. Match the engine on the window sticker and the VIN decode to the active RAM chart rather than relying on a general engine label.

Payload and tongue weight matter

A 10,000 lb trailer commonly creates substantial loaded tongue weight. That tongue weight, passengers, gear, and the hitch all count against payload. Use the payload sticker on the individual truck as the final check.
