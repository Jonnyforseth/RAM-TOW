GVWR and axle ratio can change the answer.

A RAM 2500 6.4L HEMI can have different towing and payload ratings even when the trucks look similar. Cab, bed, drivetrain, axle ratio, GVWR, receiver or fifth-wheel setup, and the actual payload label all matter.

Why a range may appear

Some window stickers do not clearly list every chart-critical GVWR or axle detail. When that happens, RAM Tow shows the applicable range rather than pretending to know the exact rating.

Use the door sticker

Select the confirmed door-sticker GVWR or axle ratio in the refinement controls to update both towing capacity and payload when that information is available.

Payload is part of the calculation

Trailer tongue weight, passengers, cargo, hitch equipment, and accessories all use payload. The yellow payload sticker on the actual truck is the final payload authority.

Conventional and fifth-wheel towing

Use the appropriate RAM chart row and hitch guidance for the trailer connection. A conventional setup should not be treated as a fifth-wheel or gooseneck configuration.

Before towing, verify the candidate truck's VIN, door-sticker labels, hitch rating, and official RAM chart information with a RAM expert.
