RAM TOW

PRIVACY POLICY

Simple summary

RAM Tow uses the information needed to run a VIN lookup or trailer-fit calculation. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not intentionally send VINs, trailer weights, tongue weights, or pin weights to Google Analytics.

Last updated August 24, 2026. This policy explains how the public RAM Tow website handles information. It is not legal advice.

Information used by the calculator

VIN lookup

When you enter a VIN, RAM Tow sends it to its lookup service so it can retrieve available vehicle information, request the Chrysler window sticker when available, and compare the detected configuration with the applicable RAM chart. RAM Tow does not provide visitor accounts or a feature intended to save VIN lookups for later use.

Trailer Fit

Trailer weight, tongue or pin weight, hitch type, and model preference are used to create your recommendation. Those inputs may appear in the Trailer Fit page URL so the result can load, but RAM Tow's Google Analytics page-view tracking removes the query string before it is recorded.

Optional camera access

The VIN scanner requests camera permission only after you choose Scan VIN. The live camera image is processed in your browser to identify a VIN; RAM Tow does not upload or store the camera stream. You can deny or revoke camera permission in your browser settings and type the VIN instead.

Operational logs

Like most websites, hosting and service providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, and error information to secure, operate, and troubleshoot the site.

Analytics and third-party links

RAM Tow uses Google Analytics 4 to understand general site use, such as page views, VIN lookup completions, Trailer Fit starts, and Trailer Fit result loads. The custom events send limited categories such as chart year, RAM model, hitch type, model preference, and result count. They do not send the VIN or trailer, tongue, or pin-weight amounts.

VIN lookups rely on external vehicle-information services, including Chrysler's window-sticker service and the NHTSA VIN decoder. Inventory and delivery links can take you to Perkins Motors, RunBuggy, RAM, or other third-party sites. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

Your choices and questions

You may use the calculator without camera access. You can control cookies and analytics through your browser settings and any available browser privacy tools. Do not enter personal information into the VIN or trailer fields.

For a question about this policy or the calculator, contact a Perkins RAM Expert at 719-249-2040. For towing and payload safety information, also review the RAM Tow Disclaimer and confirm any final towing decision with the applicable RAM materials and a qualified RAM expert.
