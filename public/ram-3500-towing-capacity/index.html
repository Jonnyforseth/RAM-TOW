RAM 3500 Cummins payload

Payload is often the controlling number for a fifth-wheel or gooseneck. A RAM 3500 Cummins may have plenty of trailer capacity while the pin weight, passengers, cargo, and hitch still require more payload. Check the yellow payload sticker on the actual truck.

SRW vs. DRW towing

Single-rear-wheel and dual-rear-wheel RAM 3500s have different chart rows and different practical payload considerations. RAM Tow shows the decoded rear-wheel setup when the sticker provides it and prompts for confirmation when a chart detail is missing.

Why axle ratio and GVWR matter

Those two details can change both towing capacity and payload. The VIN lookup matches sticker data to the active RAM tow charts; where the window sticker leaves a variable unresolved, use the door sticker or dealer confirmation to select the exact configuration.
