RAM 3500 CONFIGURATION

SRW VS. DUALLY TOWING

Rear wheels are a chart-critical detail.

A RAM 3500 single-rear-wheel truck and a RAM 3500 dual-rear-wheel truck do not share one universal towing or payload rating. Rear-wheel configuration, engine, axle ratio, GVWR, cab, bed, drivetrain, and hitch connection all need to match the applicable RAM chart.

Why a dually can be different

Dual rear wheels change the truck configuration and are commonly selected for demanding fifth-wheel or gooseneck work. The correct rating still depends on the exact chart row, not simply on the presence of a dually bed.

SRW is not automatically insufficient

A single-rear-wheel RAM 3500 may be an appropriate answer for many trailers, but both trailer capacity and loaded pin or tongue weight must fit the individual truck.

Payload often controls fifth-wheel fit

Heavy pin weight plus passengers, cargo, hitch hardware, and accessories can use available payload before a maximum tow number is reached. Confirm the actual payload label.

VIN and sticker verification

Use VIN lookup to identify the engine, rear-wheel configuration, cab, bed, axle information, and potential GVWR choices. Confirm unresolved details on the door sticker or with a RAM expert.

For a safe buying decision, compare the individual truck's VIN, labels, and installed hitch equipment to the relevant RAM chart before towing.
