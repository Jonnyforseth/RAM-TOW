How RAM Tow Calculates Towing Capacity

RAM Tow turns the truck details you can verify into the applicable model-year RAM towing and payload chart rows. It is designed to make the research process clearer, not to replace the truck's labels, the RAM chart, or qualified confirmation.

Current coverage

Supported RAM model years

VIN Lookup currently supports 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 RAM 1500, RAM 1500 Classic, RAM 2500, and RAM 3500 pickup trucks. 2027 RAM 1500 pickups are also supported; 2027 RAM 2500 and 3500 charts are not yet loaded. Chassis-cab models are not supported. Each model year uses its own loaded RAM customer towing and payload chart data. A newer chart is never used to rate an older truck.

Source charts

Two official chart sets per year

For each supported year, RAM Tow references the RAM 1500 customer payload and towing chart plus the RAM 2500/3500 Heavy Duty customer towing and payload chart. The active source sets are the 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 releases supplied with this calculator.

Review RAM towing basics and terms

VIN lookup workflow

Match the whole truck, not just the badge.

RAM Tow reads the VIN's model year and works with the available Chrysler window sticker. It identifies or narrows the model, engine, drivetrain, cab, bed, axle ratio, GVWR, rear-wheel setup, and trim details that can affect a chart row. The result is compared only with rows from the correct model year.

When details are missing

Ranges are intentional, not guesses.

Some window stickers do not clearly show the axle ratio, GVWR, or another chart-critical detail. When that leaves more than one valid RAM chart row, RAM Tow shows the lowest-to-highest possible range and provides available configuration choices. Selecting a confirmed GVWR or axle ratio recalculates both towing capacity and payload together.

RAM Tow accuracy promise

Clear truck details get a clear chart answer.

When the sticker confirms the chart-critical details: RAM Tow shows the matching model-year towing capacity and chart payload result for that configuration.

When the sticker leaves a key detail unclear: RAM Tow shows the honest lowest-to-highest chart range and keeps the GVWR, axle ratio, bed, or RamBox choices available so you can refine the result with the truck's physical labels.

Before you tow: confirm the final rating, door-jamb payload label, hitch equipment, trailer loading, and any unclear configuration detail with the applicable RAM materials and a qualified RAM expert.

Trailer Match workflow

Start with the real loaded trailer.

Trailer Match compares loaded trailer weight plus tongue or pin weight against chart-backed RAM configurations. It considers hitch type and both towing and payload requirements, then presents practical starting points rather than claiming that a specific inventory unit is approved without VIN verification.

Final verification

What the calculator cannot replace

RAM Tow cannot inspect the physical truck, its door-jamb payload label, installed hitch equipment, aftermarket accessories, passenger and cargo load, trailer loading, or scale weights. The final decision must use the applicable RAM towing chart, payload sticker, hitch instructions, owner materials, and direct confirmation from a qualified RAM professional.

Use the calculator as your starting point.
